# Insight rules

## Levels
| Level | What | In the letter? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Summary | "You wrote 12 notes about memory" | Stats line only |
| 2 Stats | "9 saved, 1 read" | Stats line / hoarding support |
| 3 Connection | "Friday's note relates to 'three drawers' from October, not linked" | Yes |
| 4 Contradiction | "On Tuesday you wrote X; this week you did not-X" | Yes, with both quotes |
| 5 Direction | "6 weeks circling this topic without a conclusion or published piece" | Yes |

## Good vs bad
- Bad: "Main theme: AI." (the user knows)
- Good: "Memory and forgetting dominate Obsidian and Notion, but nothing was published in 3 weeks — the essay is ready except for the writing."
- Bad: "You seem distracted." (judgement, no evidence)
- Good: quote the user's rule ("one topic per week") next to the measured week (22 books, 13 clippings saved), then ask whether the rule still holds.

## Anti-hallucination
- A contradiction needs two pieces of evidence: an exact quote and either another exact quote or a count you actually measured. Link both.
- Counts from file names or sizes are estimates — say so.
- If a source could not be read, say which and leave it out.
- Do not infer feelings, health, or private matters from notes. Stay with ideas and behaviour the notes show.
